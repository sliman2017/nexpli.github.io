1 Introduction

Welcome to Nexpli – Instant Text Summarizer ("Nexpli", "the Extension", "we", "our", or "us").

Nexpli is an AI-powered Chrome Extension that helps users summarize, translate, explain, rewrite, and improve text selected by the user.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our Extension.

By using Nexpli, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2 Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

When you sign in using Google Authentication, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Google account identifier
  • User ID

This information is used to manage your account, usage limits, and subscription access.

Selected Text Content

Nexpli processes only the text that you explicitly select and send for an AI operation, including:

  • Summarization
  • Translation
  • Explanation
  • Text rewriting
  • Text polishing

We do not automatically collect, scan, or store the content of webpages you visit.

Usage Information

We may collect:

  • Number of AI requests made
  • Feature usage information
  • Subscription status
  • Extension preferences

This information helps us manage free limits, Pro access, and improve the service.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide AI-powered text processing features.
  • Generate summaries, translations, explanations, and rewritten text.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Manage subscriptions and access to Pro features.
  • Maintain service security.
  • Improve performance and reliability.

4 AI Processing

When you request an AI feature, your selected text is securely processed through our AI service provider: OpenRouter.

The text is used only to generate the requested response.

We do not use your submitted text to create public content or share it with other users.

5 Payment Information

Paid subscriptions are processed securely through Lemon Squeezy.

Nexpli does not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Credit card numbers
  • Banking information
  • Payment credentials

Payment information is handled directly by the payment provider.

6 Third-Party Services

Nexpli uses trusted third-party services, including:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • OpenRouter
  • Lemon Squeezy

These services process information only as necessary to provide their functionality.

7 Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your personal information.
  • Rent your personal information.
  • Share your information for advertising purposes.

Information is only shared with service providers required to operate Nexpli.

8 Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect user information.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9 Data Retention

We retain account and subscription information only as long as necessary to provide the service.

Selected text is processed only when requested by the user and is not permanently stored unless required for service functionality.

10 Your Rights

You may:

  • Use Nexpli without creating an account (with limited features).
  • Delete your account by contacting us.
  • Cancel your subscription at any time.

11 Children's Privacy

Nexpli is not intended for children under 13 years old.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
